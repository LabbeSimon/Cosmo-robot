### Full description (2183 / 4000 characters)

```text
Give your Anki Cozmo a second chance. Cosmo Robot drives the robot straight from your Android phone, without the official app, without an account and without any server.

The app joins the robot's own Wi-Fi and talks to it directly. Nothing else is involved: no sign-in, no cloud, no telemetry.

WHAT IT DOES
• Connect from the app: type the password Cozmo shows on its face. On Android 10 and later, your phone keeps its internet connection for your other apps.
• Drive with an analogue joystick. Safety stop: if the app freezes or your finger slides off the screen, the robot stops within 0.35 seconds.
• Set the head angle, lift height, speed and volume.
• Watch Cozmo's camera live.
• Make Cozmo speak: type a sentence and the robot says it with your phone's text-to-speech voice, in any language your phone has a voice for.
• Show your own text on Cozmo's screen.
• Change the colour of the backpack lights.
• See the battery level, and whether the robot is on its charger, charging, picked up or at an edge.
• Automatic reconnection when the robot reboots, a frequent event with an old battery. Head, lift and lights go back to where they were.

HOW TO CONNECT
1. Put Cozmo on its charger.
2. Raise its lift by hand and lower it again: the Wi-Fi name and password appear on its face.
3. Type the password in Cosmo Robot and tap Connect.
On Android 8 and 9, join the Cozmo Wi-Fi network in the system settings first, then tap Connect.

PRIVACY
No data leaves your phone, except to your robot. No account, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporter. The app does not ask for your location, microphone or storage.

REQUIREMENTS
• An Anki Cozmo robot and its charger (Vector is not supported).
• Android 8.0 or later, 64-bit phone.

PLANNED
Original animations (downloaded on first launch, never included in the app), face tracking, cubes, hand gestures and a surveillance mode.

OPEN SOURCE
Cosmo Robot is free software under the European Union Public Licence (EUPL-1.2), built on pycozmo (MIT). Source code, documentation and issue tracker: https://github.com/LabbeSimon/Cosmo-robot

Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Digital Dream Labs or Anki. Cozmo is a trademark of Digital Dream Labs.
```

## Google Play, other fields

- **Category:** Tools.
- **Data safety form:** the app collects no user data and shares none.
  Everything it handles (the robot's camera, the text it speaks or displays)
  stays on the phone or goes to the user's own robot over its local Wi-Fi,
  which Google's definition does not count as collection.
- **Contact e-mail and privacy policy URL** are required by the Play Console;
  the [Privacy](../README.md#privacy) section of the README can serve as the
  policy text.
